Marketing one medical institute to two very different markets

I audited its website, restructured how its courses were presented, and built its search, social and lead-generation work around one idea: these students are not one audience.

The challenge

Fourteen programs, one generic message

The website listed every program together. Several exam courses shared one destination, the AMC page had no eligibility details or FAQs, and basic SEO elements were missing or misspelled. A doctor preparing for Australia and a nurse looking for an ECG workshop landed in the same place and got the same pitch.

What I owned

The full scope, from the audit to the lead flow, as a freelancer brought in through a referral:

  • Website audit, site map, homepage wireframe and course-page template
  • SEO fixes, keyword mapping and a blog plan
  • Social media strategy, content calendar and course creatives
  • Google Ads and Meta Ads structure, including A/B tests
  • WhatsApp enquiry flow and lead tracking
